## 🚀 Summary
Restream Studio for Windows is a browser-based, cloud-powered live studio built for creators who want a professional broadcast without complicated setup. Everything runs online, so there is no heavy software to install; you open the studio in your browser and get straight to producing. With a restream live studio at your fingertips, you can bring in guests, layer on branding, and push a single show to many destinations, all from one screen.

## ✨ Feature Set
1. Broadcast one live show to YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and many more destinations at the same time 📡
2. Invite remote guests onto your stream through a shareable browser link 👥
3. Brand your broadcast with a logo, custom backgrounds, banners, and lower-thirds 🎨
4. Share your screen and play slides, demos, or clips inside the layout 🖥️
5. Gather chat from all connected platforms into a single unified view 💬

## 📥 Installation Steps
1. Click the **Download** button above to open Restream Studio in your web browser.
2. Sign up for a free account or sign in to your existing Restream account.
3. Open the Studio from your browser dashboard and start producing; on Windows you can also use the desktop app where it is available for your plan.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open the studio and name your broadcast.
2. Connect the streaming destinations you want to reach.
3. Turn on your camera and microphone and choose a layout.
4. Add branding and invite guests with the join link.
5. Click **Go Live** to broadcast to all your channels at once.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Restream Studio free?**
Yes. Restream Studio has a free plan that covers the basics of going live, plus paid tiers that add more destinations, higher quality, and advanced branding options.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
It runs entirely in your browser, so Restream Studio works on Windows 10 and Windows 11 with any modern, updated browser.

**Is my data secure?**
Restream protects your account and broadcasts on its cloud platform, and you decide which channels to connect and what to stream live.
